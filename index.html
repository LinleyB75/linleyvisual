Email Automation

Emails that send themselves at the right moment. I plan, build and test automated flows in your email platform, from the trigger to the final email.

Triggers

The event that starts a flow, such as a signup, purchase, abandoned cart, link click, tag or date.

Delays

How long the flow waits between emails, such as 1 hour after a cart is left or 3 days after signup.

Conditions

Splits the flow by what a subscriber did or who they are, such as opened or not, new or returning.

Exit Rules

What ends the flow, so people stop getting emails once they buy, reply or unsubscribe.

Available triggers and conditions differ between platforms, so I build each flow to what yours supports. Tell me what should trigger your flow and what should happen next.

The Way I Work

I focus on clean code and reliable delivery. I do not do copywriting, marketing, or strategy. I solve your technical bottlenecks.

My process is simple and task-based, acting as a technical pair of hands for your email marketing projects.

My Streamlined Handoff Process

  • 1. Discuss & Handoff: We talk through your project, goals and brand needs. You send your design files and assets and I build to your specifications.
  • 2. Hand-Coded Build: I code and build the email in HTML/CSS, MJML or Maizzle, adding dynamic templating in Handlebars, Liquid or Django/Jinja-like syntax where it needs personalised content, to match the design as closely as each email client allows.
  • 3. Test & Minify: I test the code across major email clients and devices, fix what breaks, then minify the final file to keep the size down and avoid Gmail clipping.
  • 4. Review & Delivery: You review the tested email, request any agreed changes and approve it. You then receive a zipped folder with clean HTML and assets, ready to upload and send, or set up in your ESP, including as an automated flow.
