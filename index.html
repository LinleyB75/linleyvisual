What I Fix

API & Data Design

I define what data your application sends to your email platform: the JSON fields, their names and types, which are required, and the fallbacks. I write the field table your developers build against, and test it with missing, very long and special-character data before it reaches a customer.

Architecture Decisions

I help decide how the pieces fit together: what the template reads, who creates the links and tokens, who owns the API key, and what happens when a send fails. It is written down, so your team knows who owns what.

Troubleshooting the Whole System

I trace a problem through the whole flow, from your application's data to the template, the platform and the inbox, so I fix the cause and not just the symptom: empty merge tags, wrong data formats, links that don't track, blocked images, and templates that change after ESP upload.

ESP Integration & Setup

I prepare your sending account, upload templates, tag links with UTMs and run live test sends, so merge tags still work after upload.

Email Deliverability

SPF, DKIM and DMARC records set up and checked on your domain, plus a check of the template itself for common causes of delivery problems, such as broken links, oversized HTML and a missing unsubscribe link in marketing emails.

QA Testing

Every email is tested across major email clients and devices, with accessibility checks, before delivery.

Debugging & Fixing

I find and fix Outlook rendering bugs, dark mode inversion, Gmail clipping, broken spacing and images, and merge tags that break after upload.

Transactional Emails & Developer Handoff

I build and set up transactional emails: the automatic messages your system sends when someone signs up, buys, books or needs to confirm something. I can also update the templates you already have.

Each email is built in HTML/CSS or MJML, tested in major email clients, and set up in your email platform.

How the email gets sent

  • Hosted templates (such as Brevo): your developers send the email by passing the template ID and a JSON payload. Your team can update the wording without touching your application's code.
  • Flows and automations (such as Klaviyo): I build the template and set up the flow you've planned, triggered by the event your platform receives.
  • API-triggered sends: where your developers call the platform's API, I offer a small Node.js reference implementation of the send step for them to adapt. Your developers deploy and run it.

What I build

  • Emails I build: account setup and welcome emails, email verification and double opt-in, password resets and 2FA codes, order confirmations and receipts, booking and class confirmations and reminders, and account notifications.
  • Updating existing templates: fixing rendering problems, adding dynamic content, or adapting a template you already use, then re-testing it before it goes back into your platform.
  • HTML/CSS and MJML coding: layouts built to hold up across email clients, including Outlook's Word engine.
  • Dynamic content: personalisation written in your platform's own tag language: merge tags, Liquid or Handlebars. That includes fallbacks for missing data (for example "Hi there" when there's no first name), and conditional blocks and loops where your platform supports them.

Testing, setup and handoff

  • Testing: major clients including Outlook, Apple Mail, Gmail and dark mode, plus test sends with awkward data such as missing or very long names.
  • Platform setup: the template set up in your email platform, as a hosted template or inside the flow or automation that sends it, with its merge tags or parameters configured.
  • Developer handoff: the template ID or event name, a table of the fields it needs (name, format, required or optional) and, where the platform uses an API, a sample JSON request.
  • Domain authentication: SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and checked. I need access to your domain's DNS.
  • Payload checks and tests: a field table plus scripted test cases for missing, very long and special-character data, run before the template goes into your platform.

Another platform? I'll build to its tag format. What I don't do: connect your website, shop or application to your email platform. I write the template and your developer connects the data.

The Way I Work

I focus on reliable delivery and the whole path to the inbox, and handle the technical side of your email: platform setup, testing and fixes. I look at the whole system behind the email, so I fix causes, not just symptoms. I also map how your application, your email platform and your customers' inboxes connect, so the right details reach the right person. I do not do copywriting, marketing or strategy.

My Process

  • 1. Discuss & Handoff: We talk through your project and brand needs. You send your design files and assets, and I build to your specifications.
  • 2. Build: I build the email in HTML/CSS or MJML, adding Handlebars or Liquid where it needs personalised content.
  • 3. Test & Minify: I test across major email clients and devices, fix what breaks, then minify the file to avoid Gmail clipping.
  • 4. Review & Delivery: You review and approve the tested email, then receive a zipped folder of HTML and assets, ready to upload and send or set up in your ESP, including as an automated flow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer design-to-code services from Figma, Sketch, or Photoshop?

Yes. I convert designs from Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, Sketch, and image files into responsive HTML email templates that match the design as closely as each email client allows. I offer white-label services for digital agencies, as well as direct support for small businesses, individual entrepreneurs, designers, and developers.

Why hire an Email Developer over using a drag-and-drop builder?

Drag-and-drop builders definitely have their place and usage, especially for design mockups, rapid prototyping, and building simple to intermediate layouts. However, when scaling or building complex designs, they can generate bloated code that gets clipped in Gmail, breaks in legacy Outlook versions, or scrambles in Dark Mode. Custom coding overcomes these limits, delivering consistent rendering across major email clients and unlocking advanced dynamic data loops (Liquid, Handlebars).

Which ESPs and dynamic templating languages do you support?

I work with platforms including Mailchimp, Brevo, Klaviyo, MailerLite and Kit, implementing custom dynamic templates using Liquid and Handlebars.

How does hiring an Email Developer in Mauritius benefit global clients?

Operating in GMT+4 provides practical time-zone overlap with Europe, the UK, Australia, and early US East Coast mornings. This allows for smooth daily hand-offs and fast overnight turnarounds for Western agencies and international clients.

What transactional emails do you build?

I build account setup and welcome emails, email verification and double opt-in, password resets and 2FA codes, order confirmations and receipts, booking and class confirmations and reminders, and account notifications. I can also update transactional templates you already have.

How is a transactional email sent from the template you build?

It depends on the platform. On platforms that send from hosted templates, such as Brevo, your developer sends the email by passing the template ID and a JSON payload, and your team can update the wording without touching your application's code. On platforms that send from flows or automations, such as Klaviyo, I build the template and set up the flow you've planned, triggered by the event your platform receives.

Do you write the backend code that sends the emails?

I don't build or run your live system. I write the template, set it up in your email platform and give your developer the template ID or event name, a table of the fields it needs and, where the platform uses an API, a sample JSON request. I also offer a tested Node.js reference implementation of the sending step, so your developer has working code to adapt. Your developer deploys it, stores the security tokens and connects your website or application.

Can you update a transactional template we already have?

Yes. I can fix rendering problems, add dynamic content such as a fallback for a missing first name, or adapt a template you already use, then re-test it in major email clients before it goes back into your platform.

What do you test in a transactional email before it goes live?

I test the template with missing, very long and special-character data, check the payload against the field table, and check the delivered email in major email clients, including dark mode. No test covers every client, so tell me which ones your customers use and I'll check those first.
