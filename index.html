About me

Hello, I'm Linley Bignoux, originally from Melbourne, Australia. I'm an English teacher by profession, and I have a big passion for photography. Before teaching, I worked in IT and logistics for 18 years all up.

I take photos of landscapes and the places I travel to, everyday life on the street, nature and wildlife, and the world under the water. In the past I was a certified Scuba Instructor and Divemaster, and underwater photography is a big part of what I do.

Anywhere the camera takes me is good.

The cameras I use

  • Nikon D700
  • Nikon D300
  • Olympus TG-1
  • Canon 7D in an Ikelite underwater housing
  • A mix of Nikon and Tokina prime and macro lenses

You can see more of my photos on 500px and Flickr. You can also find my English lessons on Classgap and my email development work on GitHub.
