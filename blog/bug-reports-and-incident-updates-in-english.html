Writing a Clear Bug Report or Incident Update in English

Published 13 November 2026 • By Linley Bignoux

english-for-developerswritingbug-reportsincidentsvocabulary

The situation

When I worked as a developer, dark mode changed the colours of a newsletter I had built: the button, the footer and the social icons. It looked fine in one email application and broken in another. To get a problem like that fixed, the report has to say exactly where it happens, what you see, and how someone else can see it too.

Operations has the same problem: a scanner fails at one warehouse, a report shows the wrong total for one region, an order system rejects one supplier's file. "It doesn't work" helps nobody.

Why it matters

  • A clear report gets fixed faster. The person fixing it doesn't have to come back with questions.
  • A vague report gets closed with "can't reproduce".
  • Clear incident updates stop panic, because people know what's affected and when they'll hear more.

How to write a bug report

PartExample
Title: what and where"Footer text turns black in dark mode on Mac Mail"
Where"Mac Mail on macOS, dark mode on. Not on iPhone."
Steps to reproduce"1. Turn on dark mode. 2. Open the March newsletter. 3. Scroll to the footer."
Expected"White text on a grey background."
Actual"Black text on a grey background. It's almost unreadable."
How often"Every time."
Impact"Customers can't read the unsubscribe link."

Write the steps as short instructions in the imperative: "Open… Click… Enter…".

How to write an incident update

For a live problem, send short updates with a status and a time for the next one:

  1. Investigating: "We're aware that some customers can't log in. We're investigating. Next update at 10:30."
  2. Identified: "We've found the cause: an expired certificate. We're replacing it now."
  3. Monitoring: "A fix is in place, and logins are working again. We're monitoring."
  4. Resolved: "The issue is resolved. Logins were affected from 9:05 to 10:40."

Key vocabulary

WordMeaningExample
reproducemake the problem happen again"I can reproduce it on Android."
intermittenthappens sometimes, not always"The error is intermittent: about 1 in 5 attempts."
workarounda temporary way around the problem"The workaround is to export the file again."
regressionsomething that worked before and broke after a change"It looks like a regression from Tuesday's release."
root causethe real, underlying cause"The root cause was a missing date format."

A full example: weak and strong

Weak:

"Problem with colours. Since yesterday the footer is all wrong, I tried many times, please fix urgent."

Strong:

Title: Footer text turns black in dark mode on Mac Mail

Steps: 1. Turn on dark mode on macOS. 2. Open the March newsletter in Mail. 3. Scroll to the footer.
Expected: white text on grey.
Actual: black text on grey, almost unreadable.
How often: every time. Not on iPhone or Gmail.
Impact: customers can't read the unsubscribe link. Screenshot attached.

Common mistakes

MistakeInstead ofSay
No detail"It doesn't work.""The Save button does nothing when I click it."
"Since" with the present simple"Since yesterday the button is broken.""The button has been broken since yesterday."
Vague frequency"I tried many times.""It happens every time." / "3 times out of 5."
Emotion instead of impact"Please fix urgent!!""Customers can't pay, so this is urgent."
Mixing two problemsOne report with three bugsOne report for each problem

Try it yourself

Take a problem from your work and write it as a bug report with all seven parts. Then give it to a colleague and ask: could you reproduce this without asking me anything?

Frequently Asked Questions

What should a bug report include?

A specific title, where it happens, the steps to reproduce it, what you expected, what actually happened, how serious it is, and a screenshot or log if you have one.

What does steps to reproduce mean?

The exact actions someone needs to take to see the problem themselves, written as short numbered instructions: Open the app. Go to Settings. Tap Save.

How often should I send incident updates?

At regular, stated times, even when nothing has changed: Next update at 2 pm. People worry less when they know when they will hear from you again.
