Frequently Asked Questions

What does this blog cover?

The English you need at work: meetings, stand-ups, project updates, code reviews, bug reports, explaining problems, processes and decisions, giving bad news, presenting numbers, asking questions and writing emails. Each post explains one situation, why it matters, how to handle it, the key phrases, a full example and the common mistakes.

Who is this blog for?

Professionals who already use English at work and want to sound clearer and more confident in it: developers and other tech professionals, and people in logistics, finance, sales, engineering and management.

Are the examples from real work?

Yes. The situations come from my own work as a developer and my 18 years in stock control and inventory, generalised so that no client or employer is identified.

Can I practise these situations with you?

Yes. I teach one-to-one online lessons where we practise these situations with your real work. You can start with a free 20-minute trial lesson.
