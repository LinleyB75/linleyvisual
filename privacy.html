Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This page explains what information linleyb.com collects and how it is used. The site is run by Linley Bignoux.

What I collect

  • If you email me, I receive your email address and what you write. I use it only to reply to you.
  • If you accept cookies, Google Analytics counts visits and records general information such as the pages you view, how you found the site, your type of device and your approximate location. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Google Analytics cookies (their names start with _ga) are set only after you accept. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.
  • Your browser keeps two small settings for this site: your cookie choice and, if you use the switch, light or dark mode. They stay on your device and are not sent to me.

Services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept cookies. See the Google privacy policy.
  • The icons come from Font Awesome through cdnjs, run by Cloudflare. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Your choices

You can decline or withdraw analytics cookies at any time. You can also ask me what personal information I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it.
