Transactional Email Development for Your Application

Who this is for

Teams with their own application and a developer who owns the backend: a SaaS product, a marketplace, an online school, an online shop. Your application sends receipts, sign-ups, password resets, bookings and two-factor messages, and you want each one to arrive, to render properly, and never to show an empty field.

A good fit

  • Your application sends emails when something happens, such as an order, a sign-up or a failed payment
  • Your developer would rather not write email HTML, dark mode and Outlook fixes
  • You use, or are choosing, a provider that sends from a stored email by its ID
  • You want to change the wording without a deployment

Not a fit

  • Marketing campaigns, newsletters and visual flows
  • Copywriting, strategy or marketing advice
  • Someone to host, connect or run your live system

The problems I fix

Empty or wrong fields

"Hi ," and "undefined" in a customer's inbox. I define each value, its format and its fallback, and refuse bad data before anything is sent.

Emails that break in dark mode or Outlook

Checked in a real browser and opened in real email clients, in light and dark mode, with the parts clients disagree about written by hand.

A developer losing days to email HTML

They receive a finished, checked email and a tested send step to adapt, so the work is connecting it, not building it.

Wording changes that need a deployment

The email lives at your provider and is found by its ID. Your team edits the wording there.

Problems nobody can trace

Every link in the chain has an owner and a check, so a fault is traced to its link, from your application's data to the inbox.

A key or secret in the wrong place

The send step never prints your API key, refuses to send until someone types SEND, and keeps the key in your environment, not in a file.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a transactional email developer?

I design, develop, program and test the emails your application sends when something happens, such as a receipt, a sign-up confirmation or a password reset. I also define the data your application sends to fill them in, and I write my side of the API call.

Do you connect the email to my application?

No. I define the data, write my side of the API call as a tested Node.js reference, and hand it over with a one-page handoff. Your team connects it, keeps the API key and decides when each email is sent.

Which providers do you build for?

Brevo, Postmark, SendGrid and MailerSend: providers that keep the email on their side and send it by its ID, so your application only passes the data. Ask me about another one that works the same way.

How do you test an email before it goes live?

I run a dry run, a sandbox call where the provider has one, and one real send to an inbox. The email is checked in a real browser and opened in real email clients, in light and dark mode, and tested with missing, very long and special-character data.

What do you need from me to start?

The purpose of the email and what triggers it, your provider (or a short conversation to choose between the ones I build for), some sample data, and a design or brand to match. If you want SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and checked, I also need access to your domain's DNS.

Can you fix an email my developer already built?

Yes. I can audit it, fix rendering and dark mode problems, add fallbacks for missing data and re-test it. I do not take over running your live system.

Can my team change the wording later?

Yes, with providers that host the email. The email is stored at the provider and found by its ID, so your team can edit the wording there without changing your application.

How much does it cost?

Each email is scoped on its own, because the work depends on the email, the data and the provider. Send me the details and I will quote for that email.
